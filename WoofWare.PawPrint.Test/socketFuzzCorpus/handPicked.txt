# Rows 1 and 2 drain the port before the connect, and the drain is what makes
# them discriminating: an idle stream's level is OUT|HUP and HUP is
# unmaskable, so ANY ADD of an idle client pends it immediately — after which
# a re-signal does not move it and a MOD does not either, and the connect-time
# facts these rows target become unobservable. A first version of both rows
# skipped the drain and killed nothing.
#

#    interest miss, and the entry keeps the wake's position ahead of the newer

#    Keyed mutant pends the client only at the MOD and answers [4/IN,1/OUT].
